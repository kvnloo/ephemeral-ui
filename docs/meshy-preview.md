# Meshy plugin preview asset

The Hermes Meshy chain is simulated. The model shown in its card and in the
standalone Meshy 3D preview is the repository's Blender-built clockwork heron,
**not Meshy output**.

- Asset: `src/media/3d/heron.glb` (unchanged from commit `7d24f9d`)
- SHA-256: `1dd21329da6059ca1b58c15c8256783101375f976c9daab43fcc2b1be723030f`
- GLB generator metadata: glTF-Transform v4.5.1; the existing renderer records
  Blender as the source of the sample model.
- Embedded animation: “ARDEA • six-second fountain cycle”.

To replace it, obtain Kevin's approved Meshy GLB and record its original source,
creator, permission to redistribute, export details, and checksum. Verify the
replacement in the browser, including playback if the model includes animation.
